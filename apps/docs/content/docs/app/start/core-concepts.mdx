---
title: Core Concepts
description: The basic product concepts behind Agent-HTML artifacts and the app shell.
---

Agent-HTML uses a few stable product concepts.

## Artifact

An artifact is a durable AI-generated result. It should preserve the structure of
the work: claims, evidence, comparisons, decisions, risks, and next steps.

## Workspace

Workspace is the default app mode. It loads projects, opens section tabs, and
guards document navigation when a section has unsaved changes.

## Preview

Preview stays in the work loop. Artifacts remain visible during review and
revision, not only after export.

## Review

Review means reading the artifact as a shared object. The goal is to make it easy
to scan, judge, point at a section, request a revision, and continue.

## Gallery

Gallery is the app design mode. Its current views are Theme, Components, and
Pets.

## Pets

Pets are companion surfaces around workspace activity. They are App behavior,
not Runtime tags.
