---
title: Reviewing Artifacts
description: How to read and respond to Agent-HTML artifacts as shared work objects.
---

Agent-HTML artifacts are meant to be reviewed as structured work, not skimmed as
one long chat answer.

When reviewing an artifact, look for:

- the main claim or decision
- supporting evidence
- comparisons and tradeoffs
- risks and unresolved questions
- next actions

Good review feedback should point at the visible structure. Ask for changes to a
section, decision, table, comparison, or next step rather than restarting from a
blank prompt.
